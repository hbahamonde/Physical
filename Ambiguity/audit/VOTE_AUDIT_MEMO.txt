Read-only audit of dat.Rdata vote counts and candidate identity
12 October 2026

Conclusion
The saved voting variable contains two distinct errors. For every saved full name,
the saved turnout equals exactly twice that person's official municipal vote count.
In addition, 68 saved first names identify another person than the photograph's
candidate number and municipality. In 67 of those cases the mistaken person has a
different vote total. Dividing every saved value by two therefore does not fully
repair the vote variable for the photographed candidates.

Coverage and official source
The Ministry of Justice official national 2017 candidate-results archive contains
702,018 records: 668,400 polling-district records (type A) plus 33,618 municipal
candidate totals (type K). The official dictionary defines field 4's area types,
field 15's candidate number, fields 18/19's first/last names, fields 20/21's sex/age,
and field 35's total votes. Only type K records were used for the verification.
All 33,618 municipal city/candidate-number keys are unique.

All municipal results are count-phase T (verification count), status V (complete).

All 1,389 saved rows across 221 cities matched exactly one official municipality
and candidate-number record. There were no unmatched rows or duplicate keys.
Every saved surname matched that official surname exactly. Among these records,
1,322 saved counts are exactly twice the official count and 67 are not.

Photograph identity validation
All 1,389 saved IDs matched uniquely to id.xls. In every case, saved municipality
name (city), candidate number, party, and surname equal the corresponding metadata
fields. All 1,389 IDs also matched the original phys_cong.dta appearance file.
The saved age, ISCO code, occupation-congruent appearance, femininity, masculinity,
attractiveness, and fem_dom_job values remain identical to that appearance ID.
All 1,385 nonmissing saved gender and age observations match the official person
identified by municipality and candidate number exactly. The four missing
appearance profiles are Arhinmaki, Henriksson, Orpo, and Rostedt.
This verifies city + candidate_number as the photographed candidate's official
identity. The saved firstname field is not a reliable photo-identity key.

First-name discrepancy details
69 literal first-name discrepancies were found. One is only quotation escaping
(Anne ""Kikkis"" versus Anne "Kikkis"). The remaining 68 are different people
with the same surname, party and municipality. One of those two people happens
to have the same vote total, leaving 67 nonconstant vote discrepancies.
Every saved full name (with quotation escaping normalized) uniquely matches an
official person, and all 1,389 saved counts equal twice that named person's votes.

Concrete example
Photo ID 4647: Vantaa, candidate 104, surname Haverinen, male, age 39. Metadata and
official returns identify Jarmo Haverinen, 15 official votes. The saved merged row
instead says Soili Haverinen, turnout 550. Soili is candidate 105, female, age 56,
275 official votes. The saved appearance measures belong to Jarmo. Merely halving
550 would assign Soili's 275 votes to Jarmo's photograph.

Municipal denominators and election status
Summing official municipal candidate totals gives 2,570,768 valid ballots across
295 municipalities, with 8,999 elected councillors. Each matched candidate's
city_valid_votes is that municipality's sum across ALL official candidates, not
the photographed sample. The verified photo sample contains 288 elected, 312
alternate, and 789 not elected candidates. Official election-status code 1 is
elected; 2 is alternate; 3 is not elected. A vote share is a share of valid ballots,
not a probability of winning a seat.

Portable crosswalk for an in-memory repair
verified_photo_candidate_crosswalk.csv contains all 1,389 IDs, municipalities and
candidate numbers, official votes, city_valid_votes, vote share, election status,
official first/last names, party, gender, age, incumbency/MP indicators, and metadata
verification flags. Join on id PLUS city PLUS candidate_number; assert unique keys
and unchanged row order before estimation. Do not join on contaminated firstname.
The source dataset need not be recreated or overwritten.
