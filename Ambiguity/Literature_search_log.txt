Screening rules:

- Included journal articles on candidate visual gender cues and electoral performance, relevant peer-reviewed categorization/measurement studies, and tightly related appearance-election studies from the supplied article.
- Required primary publisher or published-paper evidence for substantive claims. A publisher abstract establishes only the claims/design it actually states. Repository copies were used only when clearly identifiable as the published/refereed paper.
- Excluded dissertations, conference abstracts/programmes, preprints, books/chapters, press coverage, general web pages, and irrelevant gender-in-work studies as review evidence. They sometimes served as leads.
- Did not conflate vote intention, election-result prediction, candidate impressions, actual binary winning, margins of victory, vote shares, or personal vote counts.
- Did not treat indexed upload/crawl timestamps as publication dates. For example, Bahamonde--Sarpila is final-volume 2024 despite 2023 first-online/filename; Carpinella et al. is 2016 issue/2015 first online; Naunov is final 2026 despite an aggregator labeling 2025.
